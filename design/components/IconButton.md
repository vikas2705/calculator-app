# IconButton

A 44×44 neutral glass button holding one 20px stroke icon (1.8px, round caps), used for the theme toggle, help, history and close; the Clear text button shares its look.

Fill `key-digit`, hover `key-digit-hover`, 1px `glass-border`, `radius-control`, pressed `scale(.94)`. Always an `aria-label` describing the action. The theme toggle shows the sun while dark (it switches to light) and the moon while light, and its label names the destination: `Switch to light theme (Pastel Morning)` / `Switch to dark theme (Aurora Night)`.
